Hexsweeper Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

Hexsweeper is a game by Luke Steuber. It is sold on the App Store as Hexsweeper 3D.

Data collection

Hexsweeper does not require an account and does not collect personal information. It contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no cross-app tracking.

Network use

The iOS and Android apps make no network requests at all. Every asset they need — the game code, fonts, and images — is bundled inside the app, so it works with no connection and sends nothing anywhere. The only exception is a link you tap yourself, which opens in your browser and leaves the app.

On-device data

Board progress, appearance, effects preference, theme, and board size are stored on your device so a game can be resumed. They are never sent to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

Hexsweeper does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
