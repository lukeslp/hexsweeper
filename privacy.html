Hexsweeper Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026. Updated November 14, 2026.

Hexsweeper is a free game that I make. This policy covers the web version, the iOS app (Hexsweeper 3D on the App Store) and the Android app.

Data collection

Hexsweeper does not require an account and does not collect personal information. It contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no cross-app tracking.

Network use

The iOS and Android apps make no network requests at all. Every asset they need (the game code, fonts, and images) is bundled inside the app, so it works with no connection and sends nothing anywhere. The only exception is a link you tap yourself, which opens in your browser and leaves the app.

On-device data

Board progress, best times, appearance, effects preference, theme, and board size are stored on your device so a game can be resumed. They are never sent to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app removes them from the device.

Android backup

From version 1.0.11, the Android app lets Android back up one small file: your best times, theme, board size, drag direction, effects and count style. Android keeps that backup in your own Google account backup, or moves it straight to a new phone, and the app reads it back after a reinstall. The unfinished board and everything else stay on the device. I never receive the backup. You can turn backup off in your phone’s settings.

The website

The web version at dr.eamer.dev counts page views with GoatCounter, running on my own server (stats.dr.eamer.dev). It sets no cookies. The game itself stores your board and settings in your browser’s local storage and sends them nowhere.

Children

Hexsweeper does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
