Hexsweeper 3D

Hexsweeper on a closed sphere — hex faces, six neighbors (classic Minesweeper used eight on a square grid).
Same dig-and-flag idea. First dig is always safe.

  • TapDig a face
  • Hold / ⌃-clickFlag a face
  • FlagToggle flag mode, then tap
  • DragSpin the sphere
  • PinchZoom in or out

Clear every safe face. Numbers count mines among the six touching neighbors. Solid red faces are flags.

Tap the menu button (bottom left) or anywhere outside the sphere to open the menu — seven faces of the sphere become the controls, the board stays live to spin, and theme taps preview in place: reset, undo, flag mode, board size, theme (Light, Dark, then Stratosphere, Mercury, Ion Storm, Aurora, Galaxy, Gilded Ribbon, Heat Lightning), warning style, and drag inversion. Progress autosaves. Reset or a size change starts a new sphere.
