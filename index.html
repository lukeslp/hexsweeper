How to play

Minesweeper on a honeycomb. Six neighbors max, not eight.

Goal

Light and Dark are an infinite honeycomb: the field keeps growing as you dig. Score is safe cells opened. Escape looks the same at first — then a rift awakens and tears tiles while chasing your clear frontier; keep digging ahead to flee. Fire and Plague are finite: clear every safe cell. Don't step on a mine.

Reveal

Click or tap a covered cell. First click is always safe. The number is how many mines sit on the six sides. When the flags around a number match that count, click it again (or double-click) to open the rest. In Light/Dark/Escape, the camera follows your dig and the grid expands so the canvas never runs out of hexes.

Move & zoom

Drag to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom. + / − also zoom. Space-drag or middle-drag pans.

Mine death

Stepping on a mine charges the board, then blows tiles apart with a shock ring, flying shards, and screen shake. Press R or use Restart when you're ready.

Flag

Right-click, Ctrl-click (or ⌘-click), press and hold on a phone, or turn on Flag mode (F) so every tap plants a flag.

Controls

  • Light / Dark / Fire / Plague / Escape changes theme
  • Gear holds board size, flag mode, help, restart
  • ? opens this panel

Keys
