No login, account, network connection, purchase, or special hardware is required. The app opens directly into a new sphere. Tap a face to clear it, long-press a face or enable the Flag control to place a flag, and drag anywhere on the sphere to rotate it. The ? button opens Themes, Board, and Settings; Board offers five sizes through the 2,252-face XL sphere. Progress saves automatically. The app is designed for touch-screen Fire tablets and does not support Fire TV remotes.
