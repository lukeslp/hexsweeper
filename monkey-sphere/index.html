Catch the Monkey: Orbit

Use the Arrow keys to rotate the sphere. Press Enter or Space to close the centered face. Drag to rotate with a pointer. U undoes a turn, R starts a new orbit, and question mark opens the rules.

Close the orbit

Seal all three portals before the monkey reaches one.

  1. Turn the world. Drag, or use the Arrow keys, to bring a face into view.
  2. Close one pale face. The monkey immediately takes a shortest open route.
  3. Block every portal. Coral rings are exits; the gold rim cue finds a hidden monkey.
