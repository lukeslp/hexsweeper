Hexsweeper 3D

Hexsweeper on a closed sphere — hex faces, six neighbors (classic Minesweeper used eight on a square grid).
Same dig-and-flag idea. First dig is always safe.

  • TapDig a face
  • Hold / ⌃-clickFlag a face
  • FlagToggle flag mode, then tap
  • DragSpin the sphere
  • PinchZoom in or out

Clear every safe face. Numbers count mines among the six touching neighbors. Solid red faces are flags.

The bottom seed opens Undo, Flag, Reset, board size, theme, warning style and inversion. Surface packs are just below. Progress autosaves through refresh. Reset or size change starts a new sphere (no confirm).
