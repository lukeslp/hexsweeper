Minesweeper you already know, on a board you've never seen: a hexagonal grid bent around a 3D sphere. Spin the globe, read the numbers, and clear every safe cell — there are no edges to hide against, and no corners to save you.

THREE GAMES IN ONE

• Sphere — the headline act. A Goldberg-sphere board you rotate freely while you sweep. Every cell has neighbors; the topology is the difficulty.
• Infinite — a flat hexagonal field that keeps going as far as you can survive.
• Bounded — classic fixed boards when you want a beginning, middle, and end.

MADE TO BE LOOKED AT

Seven visual themes, from a bright paper board to stark black-and-white to a luminous glow, all rendered live. The app icon is a real board from the game — and the icon follows your light or dark home screen.

PLAYS LIKE A GOOD UTILITY

• Entirely offline. No account, no ads, no analytics, nothing to accept.
• Flag with a long press. Undo a bad tap on the sphere; chord satisfied numbers on the flat boards.
• Hex grids mean every number counts up to six — cleaner deduction than squares.

Free, complete, and quiet. Just minesweeper, with better geometry.
