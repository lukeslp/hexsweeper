Hex Sphere

Minesweeper on a closed Goldberg sphere — six geodesic neighbors per face instead of a flat grid.

  • Drag to rotate the sphere.
  • Tap / click a covered face to dig. The first dig is always safe.
  • Numbers count adjacent mines. Empty regions expand in a wave over neighbors.
  • Flag suspected mines: right-click, long-press, F, or the flag button.
  • Pentagons mark the twelve icosahedron poles — still playable hexes.
  • Clear every safe face to win. Hit a mine and the run ends — reset to try again.

Size — S / M / L cycles density (362 · 1002 · 1442 faces). Theme — palette icon or T cycles visual skins (Void, Light, Dusk, Ember, Moss).
