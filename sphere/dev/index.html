Hexsweeper 3D

Hexsweeper on a closed sphere — hex faces, six neighbors (classic Minesweeper used eight on a square grid).
Same dig-and-flag idea. First dig is always safe.

  • TapDig a face
  • HoldFlag a face (mobile)
  • FlagToggle flag mode, then tap
  • DragSpin the sphere
  • PinchZoom in or out

Clear every safe face. Numbers count mines among the six touching neighbors. Solid red faces are flags.

Tap outside the sphere to open the center menu: reset, undo, flag mode, board size, light or dark, warning style, and drag inversion. Progress autosaves through refresh. Reset or size change starts a new sphere (no confirm).
