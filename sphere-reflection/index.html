Obsidian Mirror

A mirrored Hexsweeper sphere — six neighbors, first dig always safe. Covered faces hold the reflection; cleared faces become matte wells.

Reflected surface
  • TapDig a face
  • Hold / ⌃-clickFlag a face
  • FlagToggle flag mode, then tap
  • DragSpin the sphere
  • PinchZoom in or out

Clear every safe face. Mirror means covered; matte means cleared; scarlet means flagged. Numbers count mines among the six touching neighbors.

Tap outside the sphere to open the center menu: reset, undo, flag mode, board size, light or dark, warning style, and drag inversion. Progress autosaves through refresh. Reset or size change starts a new sphere (no confirm).
