Hexsweeper · Dodecahedron

Twelve connected pentagonal panels, each covered by its own Hexsweeper field. Clear a panel to open its five neighboring routes.
The first dig on every panel is safe.

  • TapSelect an unlocked panel, then dig a cell
  • HoldFlag a cell (mobile)
  • FlagToggle flag mode, then tap
  • DragSpin the solid in overview
  • EscReturn to the solid

Clear every safe cell on the active panel. A completed panel settles into the solid and unlocks its five macro-neighbors. Counts and floods stay local to the current panel.

Tap the open canvas to bloom Undo, Flag, Reset, panel density, light/dark and warning style. P19 is touch-first; P37 and P61 make each panel denser. A mine fails only the active panel, and campaign progress autosaves.
