Have you played minesweeper?

Hexsweeper is the same puzzle on a honeycomb — six neighbors instead of eight.

Light — porcelain infinite plate

Quick refresher

  • Right-click or press-and-hold to flag mines.
  • Use the numbers to figure out which hexes are dangerous.
  • Clear every safe cell — don't step on a mine.
  • Escape: dig toward the finish hex (edge cue). A slow rift follows your digs. Mines and the rift both kill.
